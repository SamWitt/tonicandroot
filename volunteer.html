Volunteer

Give a few hours, get a whole community back

Vine & Sunset runs on neighbors who show up for each other. Lend a hand for an afternoon or a whole season — we'll meet you where your time allows.

We're a small nonprofit, which means our volunteers aren't extra help — they're the reason half of what happens here is possible at all. Every open mic, every free wellness visit, every tidy row of books on the shelf has a volunteer behind it. When you give us your time, you're not just filling a shift. You're helping build the kind of place Akron keeps saying it needs.

What It's Like

Flexible shifts, real training, and a drink on us

Volunteering here doesn't mean a rigid schedule or a steep learning curve. Pick up a shift when it works for you, and we'll teach you everything you need to know — no experience required.

Most volunteers commit to a few hours a week or a couple of events a month, whatever fits. Before your first shift, our team walks you through the space, the tools, and the people you'll be working alongside. And while you're on shift, a free elixir or cup of tea from the bar is always on us — because you should feel as taken care of as our guests do.
