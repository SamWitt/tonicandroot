Book & Gift Shop

Shelves worth getting lost in

Pull a book off the shelf, sink into a chair, and stay as long as you like — nobody's rushing you toward the register.

What We Stock

Books chosen with care, not by algorithm

Every title on our shelves earned its spot. You'll find herbalism guides, poetry collections, essays on mindful living, and novels and nonfiction from local Akron authors — the kind of books that reward slow reading.

The shop doubles as a reading nook, tucked into a quiet corner with soft chairs and good light. Grab an elixir from the bar, find a book, and stay a while.
