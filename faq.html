Questions & Answers

Everything you're wondering about

New to Vine & Sunset? Here are the questions we hear most, from what's in the glass to how to book a visit with Dr. Marsh.

No — every drink at Vine & Sunset is alcohol-free, always. We're not a dry version of a bar; we're our own thing entirely. Our botanical elixirs are built from real ingredients — herbs, roots, shrubs, bitters, and fresh juice — so you get a drink with depth and ceremony, not a substitute. It means everyone can pull up a stool: the sober-curious, people in recovery, pregnant guests, designated drivers, and anyone who just wants a great drink without the aftermath.

Absolutely. Bring a book, your laptop, or just yourself. Plenty of our regulars come to sit in the lounge with a cup of tea and never say a word to anyone but the person taking their order. There's no pressure to mingle — the space is built for quiet corners as much as it's built for conversation. Come as you are, and stay as long as you like.

Yes. Because everything behind the bar is alcohol-free, families are welcome throughout the day, especially during our Tuesday–Sunday daytime hours. Kids can order a fruity elixir mocktail of their own, browse the gift shop, and settle in with a picture book from our shelves. Evening events with live music tend to skew adult, so if you're planning a family visit, daytime is usually the easiest fit.

Dr. Elena Marsh, ND sees patients by appointment, so we'd love for you to book ahead rather than walk in expecting an open slot. That said, you're always welcome to stop by the front desk, ask questions, and get help finding a time that works. Appointments can be scheduled by phone at (330) 555-0142, by email at hello@vineandsunset.org, or through the wellness page.

A naturopathic physician, or ND, is trained in both conventional medical science and natural, whole-person approaches to health — nutrition, herbal medicine, lifestyle change, and preventive care. Dr. Marsh takes time to understand your full history and works alongside, not instead of, any other doctors you see. Think of it as medicine that treats the whole person and asks why, not just what.

Visit costs vary by appointment type, and our front desk can walk you through current pricing before you book. Naturopathic care isn't covered by most standard insurance plans, though some flexible spending or health savings accounts can be used. We keep pricing as transparent and community-friendly as we can, and we never want cost to be a mystery — just ask.

Well-behaved leashed dogs are welcome on our outdoor patio, and service animals are always welcome throughout the space. We keep our indoor lounge, bar, and shop dog-free out of respect for guests with allergies and for the food and drinks we serve. Bring your pup, grab a patio table, and we'll bring a water bowl.

Yes. There's a small lot behind our building off West Market Street, plus free on-street parking along the surrounding blocks. On busy event nights, the lot fills quickly, so give yourself a few extra minutes or plan to park on a side street — it's a short, pleasant walk in.

We do host private gatherings — birthdays, showers, community meetings, small celebrations — usually during our daytime hours or on select evenings. Our event space can be arranged with elixir bar service, and as a nonprofit, proceeds support our community programs. Reach out through our contact page with your date and group size, and we'll help you plan it.

We're always glad to meet Akron-area makers, artists, and small producers interested in our marketplace shelves or a spot at an upcoming event. Send photos of your work, a bit about your story, and what you'd like to sell to hello@vineandsunset.org, and our community team will follow up. We look for handmade, locally rooted goods that fit our values.

Yes. Our entrance is step-free, the lounge, bar, shop, and event space are all on one accessible level, and we have an accessible restroom on site. If you have specific accessibility needs for an event or a wellness appointment, let us know ahead of your visit and we'll make sure everything is ready for you.

Yes. Vine & Sunset is a registered 501(c)(3) nonprofit, so donations are tax-deductible to the extent allowed by law. Every gift supports our community programs, wellness access, and free or low-cost events. You'll receive a receipt for your records, and we're endlessly grateful for the support.
