Non-Alcoholic Bottle Shop

Take the good stuff home

A curated shelf of alcohol-free spirits, sparkling botanicals, and tonics, so the feeling from the bar can follow you home.

What We Stock

A shelf built with the same care as the bar

We hand-pick every bottle the way we build every drink: real botanicals, honest ingredients, nothing that pretends to be something it's not. Sparkling botanicals for celebrating, herbal aperitifs for slowing down, adaptogen tonics for feeling steady, and kombucha and ferments for that little fizz of life.

Everything on this shelf comes from small producers we trust, many of them women- or family-owned, all of them making alcohol-free drinks that taste like they mean it. Grab a bottle for tonight or a case for the whole week.
