Wellness Programs

Feel better, right here in the neighborhood

Yoga, herbalism, sound baths, and one-on-one naturopathic care — all under the same roof where you already come for a drink and a good conversation.

At Vine & Sunset, wellness isn't a separate errand you have to plan for — it's woven into the everyday rhythm of the space. Roll out a mat between the bookshelves, sip an elixir before a sound bath, or slip into a quiet room for a real conversation with a doctor who has time for you. No appointment card at a sterile clinic. Just care that fits into a life you're already living.

One-on-One Care

Real time with Dr. Elena Marsh, ND

Beyond classes and workshops, our onsite naturopathic physician offers private consultations right here at Vine & Sunset — no clinic hallway, no rushed ten-minute visit.

Dr. Marsh spends real time hearing your story before building a plan around herbs, food, and rest that actually fits your life. Visits happen in a quiet treatment room just steps from the elixir bar.
