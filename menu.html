The Menu

Drinks made to feel like effort was put in

Every elixir starts with real botanicals, not a shortcut in a bottle — and every seat at the bar is open to you.

How We Pour

No alcohol, no attitude, no pressure to order "the usual"

We build every drink the way a good cocktail bar would — fresh juice, house syrups, real herbs, slow-steeped tea — we just leave the alcohol out. That means kids, folks in recovery, designated drivers, and anyone who simply doesn't drink can order something that actually feels special.

Pull up a stool whether you're staying for one round or the whole night of live music. The bartenders know the menu inside and out and are always happy to build you something off-list.
