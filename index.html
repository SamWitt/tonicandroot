Our Mission

You shouldn't need a drink in your hand to feel like you belong somewhere.

Vine & Sunset is a 501(c)(3) nonprofit built around what actually makes people feel good — plants, books, live music, honest conversation, and a seat that's always open. We just happen to serve incredible drinks while you find it.

Onsite Care

Meet the doctor behind the counter

Dr. Elena Marsh, ND, holds office hours right here at Vine & Sunset. She blends naturopathic medicine with real conversation — no rushed ten-minute visits, no judgment about where you're starting from.

Whether you want a full wellness consult or just have a question about which elixir fits your body best, Dr. Marsh is part of the everyday rhythm of this space.
